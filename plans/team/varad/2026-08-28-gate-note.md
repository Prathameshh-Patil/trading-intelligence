## 1 · S1 admits `'N'` — the contract and its own row count disagree

**Ask: yes/no on the amendment. It is drafted and needs no editing.**

`aggressor_side` is declared `'B' | 'A'`. The data carries a third value — **`'N'`**, where no
aggressor was disseminated (auction, implied, off-book). S1's own row count of 77,532 already
includes them, **so the count and the type as written cannot both be true.** Found by Prathamesh
while cutting the fixture; left alone deliberately rather than patched by one person.

⚠️ **This section carried the fixture's numbers — 1,811 trades, 2.34% — until 3 Sep, and quoted a
version of the wording that `contracts.md` had already superseded on 28 Aug.** Do not vote from an
earlier copy of this note. The rate is **not a constant**, and one session is the wrong scale to
decide a contract on. Measured across all 19 parquets (46,034,813 trades), re-verified 3 Sep:

| scope | share of **trades** | share of **volume** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| the fixture, 2026-07-16 | 2.34% | 2.05% |
| July 2026, full month | **3.89%** | **3.65%** |
| all 19 months pooled | **2.73%** | **2.68%** |
| 19-month range | 1.15% – 5.23% | 1.21% – 5.06% |
| roll months, n=9 | mean **3.75%** | mean **3.73%** |
| mid-cycle months, n=10 | mean **1.92%** | mean **1.90%** |

**Roughly double in roll months, and the volume column is the one that matters** — delta is
volume-weighted, so the trade count only bounds the damage if unsigned trades are ordinary-sized.
They are: the two columns track within 0.2 pp in every month. **Unsigned rows are worth ~2.7% of
all traded volume, and they concentrate exactly when the active contract is switching.**

Two corrections that came out of re-verifying, both in `contracts.md` now:

- **July 2026 is *in-sample* for the 3.75% roll mean**, not an out-of-sample check on it — it is one
  of the nine. The other eight average 3.73%. Consistent, but not independent confirmation.
- **The month parquets spell it `unknown`, not `'N'`.** Filtering `aggressor_side == 'N'` on a month
  file returns **zero rows** and reads as "this isn't real" — it is the fixture that uses `'B'/'A'/'N'`.
  `s1.py`'s `SIDES` handles both; a person checking the claim by hand does not.

Taken literally by a consumer, today:

```
df["aggressor_side"].map({"B": 1, "A": -1})   ->  1,811 silent NaN  (fixture)
df.groupby("aggressor_side")                  ->  3 groups, not 2
```

**The drafted wording, verbatim from `contracts.md` — unchanged by any of the above, deliberately.**
The evidence around it was corrected; the sentence you are voting on was not, so this is still the
same yes/no it was on 28 Aug:

> `aggressor_side   category  'B' | 'A' | 'N'` — `N` means no aggressor was disseminated (auction,
> implied, off-book). It **contributes 0 to delta** and its share is **not constant**: 1.15–5.23%
> across Jan 2025 – Jul 2026, averaging **3.75% in roll months against 1.92% in mid-cycle months**.
> Consumers must handle it explicitly; it is never silently dropped. **Any consumer reporting delta
> must report the `'N'` share alongside it**, because the months where delta is least complete are
> the months where the active contract changes.

**Yes** → one commit updating the type, the fake and both consumers together, per freeze discipline.
`s1.py`'s `SIDES` map already implements exactly this, so the code is ahead of the contract and the
commit is small.
**No** → then the rows must be dropped, S1's row count changes, and volume stops reconciling with
the pull. That is the more expensive answer, which is why the recommendation is yes.

## 3 · Stage 1's kill gate is judged at 5 minutes

**Ask: agree the horizon, so Part B's thresholds get written against a fixed one.**

New today, and it is the one item here that was not carried. Measured in
[`horizon.py`](../../../services/signal-data/horizon.py); full write-up in
[`daily_updates/2026-08-27.md`](../../../daily_updates/2026-08-27.md).

**GC July 2026 is a random walk at 5, 15 and 30 minutes, and two independent measurements say so.**
Dispersion grows as √h to within 0.1% (σ = 34.53 / 59.86 / 84.68 ticks). The overlap correlation
between adjacent bars' legs is 0.659 and 0.831, against a random walk's predicted (h−5)/h = 0.667
and 0.833. Neither was fitted to the other, and both walk-forward halves scale identically while
their volatility *levels* differ by 19%.

So a longer horizon buys variance at exactly the rate that makes an edge harder to prove and returns
no structure for it. **At 30 minutes Stage 1 needs an edge 2.45× larger in ticks** for the same
significance — that is √6, and it is the whole argument.

**Stage 2 arrived at the same horizon from somewhere else entirely.** Regime survival is 92% at 5m,
78% at 15m, 54% at 30m — no `--dwell-lambda` rescues 30. One argument is whether a regime outlives
the trade it selected; the other is whether the tape's noise leaves anything to measure. They did
not know about each other.

**Proposal:** 5 minutes is the gate. 15 and 30 stay in every report as descriptive columns and are
never the thing a strategy is killed on.

**This is not mine to take alone** — it changes what §6.1's Part B thresholds are written *against*,
and Part B is a pre-commitment, so it has to be settled before the numbers go in rather than after.

## 4 · More months — the same ask as §6, now with a number

**Ask: joint call with Prathamesh. It interacts with C1, so it may be a quantfeed question.**

Today's measurement produced the uncomfortable half too: **the kill gate is underpowered at every
horizon, 5 included.** At a plausible 100–250 signals the smallest edge separable from the null is
8.65–13.68 ticks — **$87 to $137 a trade.** A real order-flow edge is one to three ticks. Proving a
3-tick edge at 5m needs **2,081 signals** against a month holding 6,253 usable bars.

§6 predicted *inconclusive* and recorded it before the work. **That is now confirmed
quantitatively, and it is worse than written:** §6 expected Stage 2's regime-strategy cells to be
thin, and Stage 1 is thin on its own, before any regime split.

One month was **$2.52**; six is roughly **$15**. At 5 minutes six months puts a 3-tick edge inside
reach. **At 30 minutes six would not be enough, and neither would twelve** — which is item 3 arriving
at the same answer from the budget side.

⚠️ **Do not settle the spend before the vendor.** If quantfeed changes what a month costs, this
number changes with it, and Shreyas's three licence emails go out Monday 31 Aug.
