## 2. Line #1 — the edge test ran, and it is the reason this meeting matters

**The gate asked for one month. It got nineteen.** And the threshold discipline it asked for was not
just honoured but extended: **eleven pre-commitments** are in
[`strategy-precommit.md`](../../plans/team/strategy-precommit.md), each committed in a git commit
before the run that reads it, each reviewed by the other engineer. The mechanism `week-01.md` §2
designed — *the git timestamp is the mechanism* — worked exactly as intended.

**The answer is negative, and it is a clean, expensive, well-earned negative.**

| Measured | Result | Against |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M1** — 72-point geometry grid, 19 months, both sides, 3,456 cells | gross EV **−0.0032 ATR**/leg | cost **+0.0423** |
| **M3** — six session phases, event windows, 107,359 legs | best phase `Asia` **+0.572 ticks** | 1.40 tick round trip |
| **M2** — vol momentum, EXPANDING vs CONTRACTING | real, monotone, **+0.004 ATR** | short by **10×** |
| **Track A** — delta/CVD/absorption families | flat against a pooled null | — |

**Gold's bracket geometry carries no premium. The surface is zero, minus the cost of trading it.**
Three independent formulations — geometry, clock, volatility — each returned a number that is either
indistinguishable from a random walk or an order of magnitude short of commission. `horizon.py`
predicted it from variance before any of them ran, and `ohlcv_edge.py` reproduced it on spot to
within 0.4%.

**So the gate's own kill condition has fired**, on data already paid for, exactly as
`strategy-split.md` §2 said it would:

> If M1 returns no positive-EV cell and M2 and M3 both land inside their own MDE, **that is three
> kill conditions firing for free.**

### The thing that survived, and the room has to decide whether it is a product

**`reach.py` is built, run, and served.** 207,984 legs, 144 cells, 8,256 rows in
`analysis/reach_table.csv`, and `apps/desktop` reads it live through `services/api`. It answers
*"in this bucket, at this bracket, how often did the target come first"* — **a base rate, not a
prediction, and not an edge.**

**A base rate does not need an edge to be true, and it does not need one to be worth $39.** That is
the pivot this gate exists to accept or refuse, and it is a genuine product question rather than a
research one:

- **For it:** the number is honest, measured on 19 months, carries its own `n`, and returns `null`
  in 2,112 of 10,368 rows rather than guessing. No competitor screen tells a gold trader that their
  70/20 bracket has historically resolved to the target 41% of the time in this volatility bucket.
  It is checkable by the trader against their own fills, which is what `calibration.py` is for.
- **Against:** it tells a trader what the tape *did*, not what to do. Everything measured says the
  EV of acting on it is negative after cost. **A product that is honest and useless is still
  useless**, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` §9 open question 1 — *is this still an order-flow product* — has
  been open since 6 Sep with Varad's and Shreyas's names on it.

**This is not a question one person should answer and it is not answered here.**

### Two measurements landed after this file was drafted, and neither moves the verdict

**Step 5's tick replay ran** — Prathamesh, [`analysis/REPLAY.md`](../../services/signal-data/analysis/REPLAY.md).
Every `p_target` in this repo is a stated *lower bound* because bars cannot say which barrier a
single bar touched first, and the served table carries `p_target` beside `p_target_max` on all 8,256
rows for that reason. The replay walked the tape: **89,646 ties over 14,736,048 leg-brackets —
0.61%** — and the tape ordered every one of them, sending 41.6% to the target against a rule that
sends all of them to the stop.

**It reconciles to `reach_table.csv` to the unit** — 89,646 by both paths, the expected figure
written down while the run was still on month 12 — which is the strongest validation any measurement
in this track has carried. **No `p_target` moves**: that is a change to `backtest.first_touch`, it is
a shared-spine change with Track A's tests as the gate, and §5 says it happens only if the number
earns it. **The band's width is now measured rather than assumed**, which is what the verdict above
was resting on.

**M4b was answered the next day** — [`analysis/M4_PATH.md`](../../services/signal-data/analysis/M4_PATH.md).
Bars can sequence MFE against MAE on **95.8%** of legs, so the Path-Dependent Exit's blocker was
never really there, and §11's kill condition fires. **That is evidence for deciding M4's
path-dependent candidate *against*** — not evidence that path information is worthless. It also
retires the row this file's §5 originally carried as the most likely way its recommendation was
wrong.

⚠️ **Step 5 was built twice, independently, on the same day.** Prathamesh's is on `main` and is the

### 4b · Does the project deserve to exist?

**Recommendation: YES, and not as the product that was specified.** Three separate reasons, in
order of weight:

1. **The research answered its question honestly and cheaply.** Nineteen months, eleven
   pre-commitments, three kill conditions fired, $64 in data, no month of building the wrong thing.
   That is the machinery working. **A project that can produce a clean negative in two weeks is worth
   more than one that cannot.**
2. **The negative is about the *strategy*, not about the *instrument or the surface*.** Nothing
   measured says a gold trader has what they need on screen. It says a bracket has no premium.
3. **But the order-flow product as specified is not supported by anything measured**, and the room
   has been carrying that tension in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §1.1 since 6 Sep without resolving it.

**The concrete proposal for the room, and it is a scope change, not a pivot:** the next four weeks
build the **forecast and journal surface** on the reach table that already exists and is already
served, and **the edge claim is dropped from the product entirely** — no signals, no "3 of 4
conditions fired", no implied direction. `calibration.py` proves the number in front of the trader.
If that is not a product, it is better to know it in Week 2 than in Week 8.
