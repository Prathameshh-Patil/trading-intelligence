## The surface is zero minus cost

3,456 cells — 72 grid points × 4 `atr_bp` buckets × 6 phases × 2 sides — over 19 months, both sides,
1h 35m, matching the projection to within 10%. Every cell has n ≥ 400.

| | ATR per leg |
| :--- | ---: |
| mean gross EV, **before cost** | **−0.0032** |
| mean cost (1.4 ticks round trip) | **+0.0423** |
| mean net EV | **−0.0455** |

**Gross EV is zero to three decimal places and the entire negative result is the commission.** It
does not move: −0.0037 / −0.0033 / −0.0026 by horizon, +0.0007 to −0.0077 by bucket. Seventy-two
brackets, six phases, both sides, sixteen months of tape, and no corner of it pays for a round trip.

That is the benchmark `test_m3_ev.py` names — *EV on a driftless random walk is about zero* — and it
is the third independent confirmation of the same fact, after `horizon.py` on GC and `ohlcv_edge.py`
on spot to within 0.4%.

## 159 cells pass, and none is a finding

Reported before the interpretation, because the pass line was committed and is not being moved.
**159 of 3,456 (4.6%) clear all three conditions.**

**The two tools that disqualify them were both committed before the run**, which is the only reason
this reads as analysis rather than as excuse-making:

- **The side mirror removes 145.** Only 68 of 3,456 cells are positive once the opposite side is
  averaged in. A cell that pays long and loses short is the archive's direction wearing a bracket's
  clothes.
- **The barrier/open split removes 13 of the remaining 14.** They lose money on trades that
  *closed* and get their entire EV from mark-to-market on legs still open at the horizon — a
  selected set, biased toward the target by construction. Mean over the 159: `ev_barrier` −0.0802
  against `ev_open` +0.1420.

What survives both is **one cell**: `(7,10] bp · London-NY · long · 3.0×/0.75× · 60m`, at
**`ev_sym` +0.0005 ATR — two hundredths of a tick** — whose own mirror fails.

**351 cells (10.2%) are undecided by the same-bar tie band** and none of them passes. They stay
undecided until `replay.py`, which is the tie-resolution argument standing exactly where
Prathamesh's `008c61f` put it.

## The passes are M3's artefact

`London-NY` is **17% of the surface and 73% of the passing cells.**

Same phase `M3_CLOCK.md` §2 diagnosed, same mechanism: `atr_bp` looks backwards 60 minutes, so at
08:00 ET the bracket is sized off quiet late-London and is too small for the 08:30 release and 09:30
open that follow. The signature is here too — **`London-NY`'s mean `p_neither` is 0.164 against
0.264 surface-wide.** A bracket that resolves more often, measured against a null pooled over phases
that resolve less often, shows a `p_target` lift. **M1 is rediscovering M3's volatility seasonality,
which M3 already showed converts to no EV.**

**On contiguity, held to the pre-committed standard.** §1 said a lone grid point is an artefact but
*"a contiguous region clearing it is a finding"*. The passes **are** contiguous — `London-NY` long
in `(7,10]` passes a solid block at `target ≥ 3.0×` across all three horizons. **That test is met
and it does not rescue the result**, because the block sits exactly where `ev_barrier` is most
negative and `ev_open` largest. The short side's block sits in the opposite corner of the grid,
which is not what a geometry premium looks like.

## The prediction, scored

- *"No cell clears the cost floor"* — **wrong on the letter.** 159 do.
- *"If one does, expect it at targets ≤ 1.0×"* — **wrong, and in the opposite direction.** The
  passes concentrate at 3.0× and 4.0×: a *low* `p_target` at a wide target leaves most legs
  unresolved, and the mark-to-market on those carries the EV.
- *The mechanism — random walk, nothing to pay for cost* — **right, and it is the finding.**

**Wrong conclusion, right mechanism.** The error was assuming a random walk implies no cell clears
an EV screen. It does not: on 3,456 cells, drift and open marks produce passes at exactly this rate,
and the pre-committed mirror and split are what tell the two apart. That lesson is worth more than
having been right.

## What it does not do

**It does not kill the vol lane.** `strategy-split.md` §2's amended line requires no positive-EV
cell **and** M2 inside its own MDE. Cells cleared. **M1 does not fire the kill condition on the
letter and it is not being reinterpreted to make it fire.** M2 is owed, and M1's surface is now the

It also does not spend the held-out half: nothing selected, tuned or fitted, so by `BASE_RATES.md`'s
reasoning it cost no out-of-sample data. **One cell at +0.0005 ATR is not worth spending it on.**
