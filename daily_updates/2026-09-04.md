## The commitments

**Three blocks, not four.** Parameters, verified to bind against `strategies.py`'s signatures with no
unfilled keyword-only arguments — checked with `inspect.signature`, **not by running anything on the
month**, so no distribution was seen before the thresholds were set:

| strategy | committed parameters | inherits the ±15–20% delta error? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `delta_outlier` | `window="120min"`, `min_bars=12`, `z=2.0` | No — a z-score divides by its own σ |
| `cvd_divergence` | `window="60min"`, `min_bars=6`, `min_slope=200` | **Yes** — `min_slope` is in contracts |
| `footprint_stack` | `ratio=3.0`, `min_stack=3` | No — a volume ratio scales on both sides |

**Shared across all three:**

- **Horizon 5 minutes**, 15 and 30 descriptive only
- **Derived on the training half only** — ≤ 2026-07-19, 13 sessions, 3,373 labelled bars
- **Median move ≥ +14 ticks** ($140) at 5 minutes, in the signal's favour
- **Hit rate is the D5 gate formula**, not a flat number: beats the *measured* null `p₀` by
  `2 × sqrt(p₀(1−p₀)/N)` at `N ≥ 100`. Part B and the gate now cannot disagree
- **N floor 100.** Below it, inconclusive — reported, not acted on. A4's 30 is the reporting floor
- **Median MAE tolerated: 20 ticks**, equal to the hard stop
- **Kill line: fails on held-out data → dead.** Explicitly not "promising but underpowered"

The +14 tick figure was chosen against the separable-edge floor — **13.68 ticks at N=100** — so the
commitment is provable at the sample size floored below it. It is also knowingly three to five times
larger than a real order-flow edge is expected to be. That tension is the point of writing it down.

## `absorption_fade` is out, by decision

Decision 2 in the file asked whether absorption should be measured at the price level rather than as a
time-bar ratio, and warned it "may delete the rule rather than tune it." It did.

- The `|delta| / bar range` proxy was reasoned about against a **15-tick** median bar — that is the
  *minute*-bar figure. The 5-minute median is **38 ticks**, measured yesterday, so the denominator is
  2.5× wider than the note endorsing the proxy assumed.
- The classical price-level form needs code nobody has written, on the critical path of a week with no
  slack.
- Three well-posed candidates beat four with one mis-specified, against a month this thin.

Decision 1 — fade or continuation — is **recorded, not taken.** When the rule returns it returns as
*two* blocks, each measured, rather than one direction assumed by whoever wrote the docstring first.

**Two consequences, and neither is closed by dropping it:**

1. **D3's 3-of-4 checker loses condition A.** Stubbing it `return False` is what D3 already sanctions,
   but that makes the combiner effectively **3-of-3 — a stricter gate than designed**. It gets reported
   that way, not quietly relabelled.
2. **The Week 1 gate line still stands.** *"The outlier detector flags the 2026-07-16 08:00 ET
   absorption hour"* — delta **+1,083** against a **47.7-point drop**. The rule named for absorption is
   gone, so `delta_outlier` has to catch it. That is a real test, not a formality: **if nothing flags
   that hour, Stage 1 does not work.**
