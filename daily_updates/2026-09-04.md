### The bug the tests caught, which is the reason to write tests first

The EMA gate originally compared `sign(close − ema)` against a **one-bar** slope. A test asserting a
falling market fails alignment blew up, and chasing it produced the actual result:

> `ema_t` always lies between `close_t` and `ema_t−1`. So `close > ema_t` and `ema_t > ema_t−1` are
> the *same statement*, and the two signs can never disagree.

The gate was a **tautology** — it would have passed every bar except an exactly flat one and filtered
nothing at all, while looking entirely reasonable in the pass-rate table. The slope is now measured
over `span` bars, where the two quantities are genuinely independent. The case it now rejects — price
rallying back above an average that is still below where it was — is a bounce inside a downtrend, and
there is a test pinning it.

Worth noting the failure mode: this would not have shown up as a crash, a type error or an
implausible number. It would have shown up as a filter that quietly did nothing.

### Two findings that are decisions, not bugs

**1 — `survival ≥ 0.92` keeps exactly one regime, and it is the quiet one.**

Measured on the training half (13 sessions), survival one bar (5 minutes) ahead:

| regime | survival | share of bars | median `cvd_slope` | persistence |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0.869 | 15.4% | +27.6 | 0.612 |
| 1 | 0.868 | 21.5% | −26.7 | 0.613 |
| 2 | **0.924** | 63.0% | **−0.199** | **0.206** |

Only regime 2 clears. The gate therefore reduces to *"trade only regime 2"* — **the regime with
essentially no directional flow**, against the two it excludes which carry ±27 contracts of CVD slope.
For a strategy family built on order flow that may be exactly backwards.

It is not a bug and not something to patch around. `regimes_2026-09-02`'s own README already says the
run is "not yet reviewed against the plot… that review is still Varad/the room's." This is the first
concrete consequence of leaving it unreviewed, and it lands on the gate the whole week reads.

**2 — `atr_min = 40` was chosen with the answer in view.**

The band is hit by the ATR gate alone, and the sensitivity is steep:

| `atr_min` (ticks) | bars | pct |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |

40 ticks sits just under the month's median ATR(14) of **41.9** (p25 33.4, p75 54.0). Picking it to

The session-edge gate, for the record, is nearly free: 5 minutes at each end of a 23-hour session is
