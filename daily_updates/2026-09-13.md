## 4. The correction, and it is the useful part of the duplication

Varad's writeup carried a section titled *"Two structural checks, because a number this convenient
deserves them"*, claiming that symmetric brackets resolving to exactly `0.500000` was evidence the
implementation was correct:

> *"A bug in `tick_bounds` — the tick window drifting off the bar window by even one bar — breaks
> both of these."*

**That is wrong, and `REPLAY.md` §4 shows why.** A long and a short entered on the same bar with
equidistant barriers are the *same two price levels*: whichever prints first is the target for one
side and the stop for the other, so pooling the two sides can produce nothing but `0.5`. **It is
forced by construction.** A tick window drifted by a whole bar would drift both sides identically
and still return exactly `0.500000`.

So the check validates nothing it was claimed to validate. Prathamesh reached the same arithmetic
from the other direction — he records nearly writing it up as *"ties are a coin flip"* — and caught
it, correctly calling it the same class of error `M3_CLOCK.md` §4 records twice: **every component
right in isolation, wrong in composition.**

**Two independent implementations produced one real result and one caught error**, which is the only
defensible thing to say for a day spent twice.
