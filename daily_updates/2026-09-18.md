## 4. Three corrections to the plan, all found by building it

**These are corrections to work written yesterday, not to the repo.** Recorded because two of them
would have produced plausible wrong numbers rather than failures.

### ⚠️ The plan's Yang-Zhang constant was wrong

It asserted `_yz_k(12) == 0.20880`. The correct value is **0.134823**:

```
n=     2  k=0.078341
n=    12  k=0.134823
n=    48  k=0.142704
n=   288  k=0.144868
n= 10000  k=0.145287     -> 0.34/2.34, the large-n limit
```

`k` never leaves `[0.078, 0.146]`, so 0.209 was never reachable. A second test pins that band, so a
mistyped formula fails rather than returning a plausible sigma.

### ⚠️ The window boundary was off by one, and the extra bar is the estimator's actual shape

First valid index is **`n`, not `n−1`**. The overnight term is `ln(O_t / C_{t−1})`, so `n` overnight
returns need `n+1` bars. An implementation answering at index 11 for `n=12` would be averaging
eleven returns and a NaN — a smaller number that reads as calm, and `r_ratio` divides by it.

### ⚠️ `D` is exempt from §7.3's monotonicity rule, and the exemption is arithmetic

Computed from `e3_cost.surface` at the archive's 1.91 bp:

```
D      $2.00   $2.60   $3.00   $4.00   $5.00
EV bp   7.03    6.01    5.33    3.62    5.00      <- minimum at $4.00, then rises
```

**§10's `R = max(2.5D, $10.00)` is the cause.** Below `$4.00` the target *floor* binds, so the
payoff ratio slides 5:1 → 2.5:1 as `D` widens and EV falls; past `$4.00` the multiple takes over and
EV climbs. The dip is the spec's own target formula, and §7.3 as written would have rejected `D`
for fitting noise. **The general rule, now in the spec: the monotonicity check assumes a parameter
enters EV once, and `D` enters twice** — as the loss leg and, through the target formula, as the win
leg. `R`'s multiple has the same property and needs the same look.

## 5. Two guards found by testing rather than by review

### 🔢 The cost model's factor of ten, pinned against two independently published numbers

`to_bp` is one division, and a draft of the design got it wrong by 10× — `$0.30` on `$3,400` read as
8.8 bp instead of **0.88** — which flipped expectancy at the spec's own parameters from +6 bp to
−2 bp and reversed the viability call. **A one-line function that can reverse a decision earns tests
against known values**, so it now carries two: one GC tick is `$0.10`, and `M3B_DRIFT.md` prices the
GC round trip at 0.3504 bp including commission, so 0.294 bp of spread must come back.

Corrected, the three venues are **GC 0.29 bp · prop firm 0.88 bp · Dukascopy 1.91 bp**, every
break-even lands at **0.19–0.38** against the spec's claimed 42%, and **the binding constraint is E1
— whether gold makes the move at all — not cost.**

### 📉 DFA needs a minimum scale of 4, and without it returns H = 14

A least-squares line through 2 or 3 points fits them almost exactly, so `F(s)` collapses toward
zero, `log F(s)` toward `−inf`, and the slope explodes. **Measured before the guard: including
`s=2` returned H = 14.0 on a series whose true H is below 0.5.** Variance-time needs no such floor
because it fits no trend. Both estimators now refuse on fewer than three usable scales — a slope
through two points is exact and meaningless.

### 📏 H is scale-dependent, and that constrains the data E0 has to deliver

The mean-reversion test measures at scales 4–32 rather than 4–256, and **the restriction is the
finding rather than a way to make it pass.** AR(1) anti-persistence is short-range; by `s=128` the
correlation has decayed and the series is a random walk again, so a pooled regression is dominated
by the long end:

```
phi = -0.6, same series    scales 4..32     dfa 0.288   vt 0.444
                           scales 4..256    dfa 0.373   vt 0.476
```

Correct, and blind to what the series contains. This is why §6 asks for separate readings at 5m and
15m rather than one pooled number.

⚠️ **The consequence the plan did not state: `h_dfa_15m` cannot be computed from 5-minute bars.**
Three bars is not three scales. §6 needs 1-minute bars or ticks, and `spot.load_bars` currently
resamples to 5min. **This lands on E0** — the granularity pulled has to support the Hurst the S13
frame declares. Flagged, not decided.

Also pinned: both estimators take **log price levels**, and passing returns shifts H by more than
0.3 — roughly the entire usable range, so the mistake is otherwise silent.
