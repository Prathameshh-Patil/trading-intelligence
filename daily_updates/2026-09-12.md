## 3. The finding: there is no conditioning structure

§11 anticipated a middle case where the per-cell table would say *where* bars mislead. **It says
there is no where.** Over the 63 non-thin cells at 15m the rate spans 7.13%–12.89% — **1.81×** — and
the axis marginals are flat: bucket 9.40–10.89%, phase 9.00–11.05%, vol_state 9.77–10.33%.

**Real and small are different claims and both are tested.** Against a single common rate: **χ² =
186.7 on 62 dof, 3.01 per cell**, 6 of 63 beyond ±3 SE. The variation is not noise. The magnitude
decides it, and the comparison is same-archive, same-module:

```
tie rate, 72 brackets (step 5)     0.014% .. 3.97%     276x
unorderable, 63 cells (M4b)         7.13% .. 12.89%    1.8x
```

Step 5 found a quantity that concentrates. This one does not — so **a path-dependent rule cannot be
rescued by restricting it to a pocket where bars are blind, because there is no such pocket.**

## 4. ⚠️ The same artefact, twice in two days

`side` is a **relabelling here, not a second observation.** A long and a short on the same bar read
the same two prices, so `unorderable` is identical across sides and long `mfe_first` IS short
`mae_first`, exactly. Pooling forces the ordering to 50/50 — which is `REPLAY.md` §4's
symmetric-bracket artefact arriving again inside the same module.

**Worth stating as a property rather than as two coincidences:** wherever a quantity is defined
relative to the trade's own direction, pooling the sides reports one observation twice and forces
any ratio to 0.5. `path_summary` and `by_cell` both drop `side` rather than summing it, and a test
asserts each does.
