Two corrections, and the second is the substantive one. `bid`/`ask` are tick-level and do not

## 1. What it measures, and the constraint that shapes it

`first_touch_from` resolves a tie **by rule**: when one bar's high clears the target and its low
clears the stop, the order is not in the bar, and ties go to the stop. That is why every `p_target`
here is a stated lower bound and why `reach.py` serves `p` beside `pMax`. This walks the trade
prints inside that one bar and says which price level printed first.

**It does not change `backtest.first_touch`**, per §5 — that is a shared-spine change with Track A's
tests as the gate, it is Varad's, and it happens only if this number earns it. `tied`/`tied_from`
mirror the `first_touch`/`first_touch_from` split so the repo still has exactly one first-touch
convention, in `backtest.py`.

```
89,646 ties over 14,736,048 leg-brackets -- 0.61%
89,646 ordered by the tape, 0 it could not
the rule sends every one to the stop; the tape sends 37,301 (41.6%) to the target
```

## 2. The finding: the nearer barrier is touched first

`P(target first)` is **monotone in the stop/target ratio** — 0.178 at 0.125, through exactly 0.500
at symmetry, to 0.649 at 2.0. The tape says what geometry predicts, which is the sensible null and
is evidence the walk is sound rather than a surprise.

**The consequence is about the rule, not the market.** `ties="stop"` answers per-archive a question
whose answer is per-bracket: roughly right where the stop is nearer, a coin flip at symmetry, and
**wrong more often than right wherever the target is nearer** — 10 of 72 brackets, 11,593 ties,
12.9%. It stays the correct conservative choice, and `REPLAY.md` §3 says why: a lower bound is a
property nobody has to remember, a per-bracket correction is a number somebody has to keep true.

## 3. ⚠️ The thing I nearly published as a finding

**At `target == stop` the pooled split is exactly `0.500000`, on 17,592 ties — 19.6% of the
archive's — and it is forced by construction.** A long and a short entered on the same bar with
equidistant barriers are the same two price levels, so whichever prints first is the target for one
and the stop for the other; pooling the sides cannot produce anything else. The mirror is exact at
all three symmetric brackets.

Read as a result it says "ties are a coin flip". It is arithmetic, not tape. Same class as
`M3_CLOCK.md` §4's two bugs — every component right in isolation, wrong in composition — and caught
by asking why a number was *exactly* round rather than approximately.

**Per side the pooled figure survives:** short 42.4%, long 40.8%. So 41.6% is not purely an
artefact; only the symmetric brackets are.
